Method and scope

Only the prose and solution entries in research.md are analyzed. Headings, URLs, and the glossary definitions are excluded. Each prose paragraph or solution-list entry is one passage (P1–P10). Passages locate the evidence; they are also the counting unit for implicit use. Explicit use is counted per occurrence.

Explicit: the term appears by name, case-insensitively. Recorded variants include singular/plural forms, “automated test”, “automated tests”, and “automated testing”; “user interface” or “UI” count as explicit UI use. The exact matching rule is displayed with each term.

Implicit: a passage describes the concept without an explicit match. These are qualitative annotations prepared with AI assistance, supported by the displayed evidence, and subject to human review. A zero means no usage was annotated under these rules; it does not establish that the concept is entirely absent.

Explicit frequency counts all direct matches, including repeated occurrences within one passage. Implicit frequency counts annotated passages, at most once per term per passage. Related terms may share a passage. Unconnected passages remain visible. The graph does not assert causal relationships between terms.
